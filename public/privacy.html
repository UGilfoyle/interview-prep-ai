Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

InterviewPrep AI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our technical interview preparation studio.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up via Email, GitHub OAuth, or LinkedIn OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and optional avatar image.
  • Practice Data: Your interview questions, transcribed speech answers, and AI-generated rubric evaluations are stored to provide performance scorecards and progress tracking.
  • API Credentials: Your Gemini API key is stored strictly within your local browser storage and is never shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, personalize, and improve your technical interview practice sessions.
  • To generate AI-powered feedback and scorecards using Google Gemini API.
  • To synchronize your session history securely across your devices.

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate with trusted providers including Google Gemini API (for rubric evaluations), GitHub & LinkedIn (for secure single sign-on), and Neon Serverless Postgres (for encrypted database storage).

4. Data Retention and Security

We employ industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS, bcrypt password hashing, and encrypted JWT tokens) to safeguard your data. You may request deletion of your session history at any time through the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@interviewprep.ai.
